What AquaPatrol Checks in the Pond

One rugged IoT device replaces multiple fragile glass meters and notebooks with reliable continuous monitoring.

🧪

pH (Acidity & Alkalinity)

Crabs need a balanced pH of 7.5 to 8.5 to molt properly and form hard shells. Extreme pH burns gills and stops feeding.

Healthy: 6.5 – 8.5
👁️

Turbidity (25 – 50 NTU)

Measures light scattering and suspended plankton density. 25–50 NTU maintains ideal natural feeding for mud crabs. Under 25 NTU triggers cannibalism & bottom weeds; over 50 NTU cuts off oxygen & clogs gills.

Healthy: 25 – 50 NTU
💧

TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)

Monitors mineral balance and salinity levels so crabs don't experience sudden osmotic shock after rainfall.

Healthy: 10k – 25k ppm
🌡️

Water Temperature

Monitors day and night temperature swings (26°–32°C) to prevent thermal stress and appetite loss in mud crabs.

Healthy: 26° – 32°C
🧠

ESP32 Microcontroller

Dual-core processor with onboard Wi-Fi and Bluetooth filters sensor noise and operates reliably outdoors in brackish crab ponds.
